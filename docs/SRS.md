# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Purpose

SmartAssess AI is an AI-powered adaptive interview intelligence platform designed to improve the interview preparation and candidate evaluation process. The platform enables students to participate in AI-driven mock interviews while providing educational institutions and recruiters with objective, data-driven insights into candidate performance.

Unlike conventional mock interview platforms that mainly provide generic scores, SmartAssess AI performs multi-modal evaluation by analyzing resumes, interview responses, communication quality, confidence indicators, and semantic understanding. The platform aims to generate explainable feedback, identify skill gaps, and recommend personalized learning paths to improve interview readiness.

---

## 1.2 Project Objectives

The primary objectives of SmartAssess AI are:

* Automate the interview assessment process.
* Generate personalized interview questions using resume analysis.
* Evaluate candidates using multiple AI-based assessment techniques.
* Provide explainable interview feedback instead of generic scores.
* Identify strengths and weaknesses across technical and communication skills.
* Recommend personalized learning roadmaps.
* Provide analytics dashboards for students, colleges, and recruiters.

---

## 1.3 Scope

The system is designed for:

* Students preparing for placements.
* Colleges conducting mock interview assessments.
* Placement cells monitoring student readiness.
* Recruiters seeking structured interview evaluation.

The platform supports adaptive interviews, resume analysis, AI-based evaluation, interview history, progress tracking, and role-based dashboards.
